# Player guide

This guide covers the basics without revealing the story or quest solutions.

## Start playing

Open the game link, enter your character name and the game password, and select
the button to enter the game. Your name appears in the game; changing it does
not create a separate save on the same browser and device.

## Move and interact

- On a PC, use **WASD** or the **arrow keys** to move. Click the world or
  minimap to walk to a destination.
- On a phone, use the on-screen movement controls or tap the world or minimap.
- Walk near a person, entrance or object and press **E** or use the on-screen
  interaction button. You can also click or tap people and objects to approach
  them. Some quests provide their own controls on screen.

## Find quests

The map marks quest guides. Speak to a guide and accept their offer to add the
quest to the journal. Follow its destination to the entrance and interact to
begin. Select a quest in the journal to help navigate, and open **Quest
steps** to see your progress. The journal scrolls when the list is long.

The side-quest list offers optional encounters with Cat. Select an entry to
find her, then speak to her to begin.

Your items and keepsakes appear in the inventory panels. Hover over, focus or
tap an item to see its name.

## Music and settings

Music starts when you enter the game. Use the music button on the action bar
or **Settings → Music** to turn it off or on. The action bar shows the current
track when music is playing.

## Your progress

Progress is saved in this browser on this device. It does not automatically
transfer to another device, browser or website address. Clearing browser data
or using a private browsing session can remove the save.

You can leave an unfinished quest and return later. Removing a quest from the
journal resets its progress; read the confirmation before proceeding.
**Settings → Restart Game** resets the whole adventure after confirmation.
