# Quest reference

**Spoilers:** this page describes quest objectives, solutions and the ending.
For a spoiler-free introduction, see the [player guide](PLAYER-GUIDE.md).

## Progress

The main journal starts empty. Speak to a guide to accept a quest; the five
keepsake quests can be completed in any order. Expand a quest in the journal for
milestones, including items already used. Completed quests stay complete until
removed from the journal.

The character begins at level 29. Each keepsake quest awards 15 XP. The hall
awards the final 25 XP when the present is collected, reaching level 30.
Optional Cat side quests can be revisited, award no XP, and do not affect the
finale.

## Main quests

### Abigail Escape

Accept Mara's quest and enter the house. Search its five connected rooms,
including the furniture in the three additional rooms, for the hidden runes.
Place all three in the diary's order to break the ward. Use **E** or Space to
investigate nearby objects, or use the **Clues & objects** panel. Progress
and collected items are saved on leaving; an open rune puzzle can be restarted
at the ward.

### Lucky Chucks

Speak to Jamie, collect drinks from the bar and put them on the table. Read
the scorecard and use the pool table to work out the locked ball case's code.
Find the cues and balls, set them on the table and investigate it again for
the pool break. Align the guide and choose controlled power; the sliders work
with mouse, touch and keyboard. Find a tool to retrieve the eight-ball from
under the cabinet, then give it to Cat.

### Thirteen

Board with Cat. Hold the matching left or right control as the carriage passes
each park item. Collected items remain between laps; missed items stay on the
track. After collecting all five and completing the ride, retrieve the bags
from the locker and give them to Cat. If the game is reloaded after completion
but before leaving, interact with the completed ride entrance to use the
awarded exit ticket.

### GoApe

Explore the platforms and collect four treasures. Use Space or **Move** when
the timing marker reaches the green zone to climb or cross, allowing it to
leave the zone between moves. Jump across gaps, light the three lanterns in
order, and present the treasures at the zip-wire. A fall or bad timing returns
the player to a checkpoint without losing progress.

### Fright Nights

Explore the seven themed maze areas. Find each area's three clues and tool,
then solve its three-step mechanism to recover its object. Wrong answers reset
that mechanism but preserve items. Discovered clues remain in the journal and
the nearby-objects panel. After finding all seven objects, use **Teleport
out** or return to the entrance gate. Position and discoveries are saved when
leaving.

## Hall of Memories and finale

Accept Elowen's quest outside the castle and enter the hall. Place each earned
keepsake at its named display. When all five are displayed, investigate the
present to complete the hall and begin the celebration. Use **Continue** or
**E** to advance the dialogue; the birthday ticket can be opened during the
celebration and later from Keepsakes. Open the present again to replay the
ending.

Removing a completed keepsake quest removes its keepsake and XP and resets the
hall finale. Removing the hall quest resets its displays and final XP but keeps
earned keepsakes. Restarting from Settings clears all saved quest progress on
the current browser and device.
